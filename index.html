[BECKER VENTURE / WEB3]

Service
Scope

We support Web3 teams across the full lifecycle—from early incubation to growth, fundraising, and exchange listing readiness—combining strategic guidance with hands-on execution.

FULL LIFECYCLE SUPPORT06 MODULES
[01]

Incubation &
Venture Building

  • Ideation refinement, product positioning, and narrative development
  • Token / protocol design support, including high-level design review and specialist coordination
  • Team formation guidance and hiring priorities across core dev, BD, community, and operations
  • Go-to-market planning: target users, distribution channels, and growth loops
  • Milestone planning and operating cadence: OKRs, weekly execution rhythm, and KPI dashboards
[02]

Fundraising Strategy &
Financing Plan

  • Funding roadmap across pre-seed, seed, private rounds, and strategic vs. financial investors
  • Deal structuring guidance for token rounds, equity, SAFE / SAFT, in collaboration with legal partners
  • Pitch support: deck strategy, storyline, metrics, and data room preparation
  • Investor targeting and warm introductions to relevant funds, angels, and strategic partners
  • Term negotiation support: valuation, unlock / vesting logic, and governance considerations
[03]

Exchange Listing
Strategy & Execution

  • Listing readiness: compliance posture, token distribution, liquidity design, and market-making needs
  • Listing plan: timeline, DEX / CEX sequencing, and regional strategy
  • Exchange package preparation: documentation, traction proof, risk disclosure, and communications
  • Coordination across market makers, liquidity providers, PR, and advisory networks
  • Post-listing support: liquidity monitoring, community cadence, and narrative stabilization
[04]

Brand, Marketing
& Community

  • Brand strategy: positioning, identity, messaging pillars, and tone of voice
  • Content strategy spanning thought leadership, announcements, launches, and ecosystem storytelling
  • Discord / Telegram structure, community programs, and ambassador strategy
  • X (Twitter) growth strategy, campaign planning, and performance tracking
  • PR and partner-marketing coordination with external agencies when needed
[05]

Business Development &
Ecosystem Partnerships

  • Strategic partnerships with L1 / L2 ecosystems, infrastructure partners, exchanges, wallets, and dApps
  • Go-to-market collaborations: co-marketing, integrations, and distribution partnerships
  • Enterprise and institutional business-development support where relevant
  • Advisor network access across technical, legal, compliance, and market making
[06]

Operations, Governance
& Long-Term Support

  • Operating model setup: budgeting, treasury policy, and reporting cadence
  • Governance planning: DAO framework considerations and community governance design, as applicable
  • Risk management: security coordination, incident response planning, and communications process
  • Ongoing strategic support for launches, upgrades, and new-market entry
